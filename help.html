Help

A quiet timer for planning focus sessions and breaks.

Start focusing

  1. Press the timer dial to start, pause or resume.
  2. Optionally enter what you are focusing on, or choose Focus beside a queued task.
  3. Open the menu to see your profile, goals, tasks, calendar, stats and sounds. On mobile, cards stay hidden until you open them.
  4. Use Edit Profile for durations, daily goals, long-break intervals, auto-start options and notifications.

Quick answers

Can I refresh or switch tabs?

A saved running timer catches up to its deadline after refresh or switching tabs. Paused time stays paused. Keep the main tab open for audio and timely alerts. Device sleep and browser suspension may delay callbacks. A late recovery credits the original focus session once and leaves the next phase idle.

Why does another timer tab ask me to wait?

One tab at a time can change this site’s saved data. Close the other main timer tab to continue here. Mini is a view of the active timer, so it does not need another main tab.

How do I keep or move my data?

Use Edit Profile → Backup → Export backup. On the destination browser or website address, choose the backup, review the preview, then restore. Restore replaces the destination data and resets the timer; Undo restore is available. See Privacy & saved data.

How do I correct an accidental session?

Open Calendar → Edit history. Choose a date or All sessions, then Edit or Delete. Undo last change restores recent edits/deletions, including after refresh. Stats and goals follow completed history; manually completing a task does not add a focus session.

Why is there no notification or sound?

Opt in to desktop notifications in Edit Profile, then check browser and system notification settings. Banners and sounds also depend on system Focus/Do Not Disturb settings. Audio needs a user action and can be muted. The app cannot promise alerts while the page is closed or the device sleeps.

Does Mini stay on top in every browser?

Where Document Picture-in-Picture is available, Mini uses it. Other browsers open an ordinary small window, which may be covered by other apps. If popup creation is blocked, keep using the main timer.

Which browsers can I use?

Use an up-to-date browser with JavaScript, local storage, native dialogs and Web Locks over HTTPS or localhost. Unsupported tab protection shows an explanation instead of risking simultaneous writers. Mini, desktop notifications and audio support vary by browser and device. This app has no offline service worker.

What if the app cannot load or save?

Retry the page and check your connection. If storage is full, export a backup before freeing space. Damaged saved data pauses startup and offers a recovery download; keep that file for manual repair. Avoid clearing site data before saving a copy.

Keyboard and touch

Space starts or pauses; R resets. Shortcuts stay inactive while typing or using a dialog or control. Use Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Calendar arrows move by day/week, and Home/End move to the start/end of the week. On mobile, open the menu to access cards and Reset layout to return floating cards to the sidebar.
