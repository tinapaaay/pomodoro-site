Privacy & saved data

This timer keeps your work in this browser. No account is needed.

Only the information you choose

The timer works as Guest without a real name, photo, email address, birthday or account. Display name, about-you text, photo and task labels are optional. Leave profile details blank if you prefer; the app uses Guest as the name.

What is saved

Your profile name, role, chosen photo, timer settings, tasks, completed focus sessions, history edits and Undo, card positions, and sound preferences are stored locally. We do not upload these records to an app server or add analytics trackers.

Backups and moving devices

Open Edit Profile → Backup to export a JSON backup or restore one. Backups contain your profile photo and task/history text; keep them somewhere private. Your data does not sync between devices or browsers. Localhost, a public website, and a custom domain each have separate storage. Export before changing address or browser, then restore at the destination.

Browser storage can be cleared or evicted, and private browsing may discard it when you close the window. Keep regular backups of work you want to retain. If saving fails, the app shows a warning; a completed session remains available for retry or export.

Cookies & browser storage

The app code does not set browser cookies or advertising identifiers. It uses localStorage for the features described below. Storage is tied to this website address and remains until you clear it or your browser removes it; private browsing may be temporary. There is no app analytics or ad tracking.

  • Timer and tab coordination: recover the active session and prevent competing app tabs. Tab coordination uses Web Locks rather than a tracking identifier.
  • Tasks, focus history and recent Undo: retain your requested planning and progress records.
  • Profile, layout, audio and notification preferences: remember the settings you choose.
  • Backup restore/recovery: preserve a previous copy so interrupted imports can recover and you can undo a restore.

Local storage is not automatically outside privacy or cookie rules. We describe it here so you can decide whether to use these saving features. Hosting and external services may have their own storage practices; the app does not control their policies. If optional tracking is introduced, this notice and any required consent controls must be updated before it runs.

Network requests

The website host receives normal requests for pages and assets. The timer requests fonts from Google Fonts and an optional sound library from cdnjs; those providers receive the network information needed to deliver their files, such as your IP address. Your task text, session history, and profile photo are not included in these requests.

Ambient recordings are served by this website and are requested when you press Play. The optional original YouTube links open YouTube only when clicked. See audio sources and licenses. Hosting and external providers may keep their own access logs under their own policies.

Notifications

Desktop notifications require your permission. A session notification may display your current task on your device. You can turn notifications off in Edit Profile or revoke permission in your browser settings.

Removing saved information

Delete individual tasks or history entries in the app, with Undo for recent changes. Undo records can retain deleted text. To remove all local records, first export anything you want to keep, then use your browser’s site-data settings to clear this website’s storage. This also removes saved timers, profile, preferences and Undo. Downloaded backups are separate files and must be removed separately if no longer needed.
